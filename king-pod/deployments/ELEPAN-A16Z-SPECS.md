# Elepan — Full Specs (a16z memo, ≤120 words)

**Elepan** is a sovereign credit and **eUSD** peg stack across Base and Scroll. Stable exits clear through gold-parity and multi-asset PSMs (**USDC, USDT, DAI, ETH, EURC**) priced by Chainlink. Credit collateral—ELE, RSS, Morpho ELE77 (~$16.5M PoR)—is loaned, never dumped. Async redemptions use **ERC-7540** queues settled by **ERC-7683** solver intents with public fill. Institutions read Chainlink-style PoR for ELE77 and Gold CDP (~$1M collateral, HF ~1.55). Mark price sits on a Uniswap **v4** eUSD hook. Cross-chain rails are dual **CCIP / LayerZero** plus eUSD Link. Doctrine: code-first balance sheet, Landing sink, no keeper dependency. **Later: ZK** circuits for wallet-bound identity, private credit attestation, and trust-minimized settlement proofs—hardening solvency without rewriting the live 7540/7683 rails.
